Europe's Industry Doesn't Want an AI That Writes Poetry
Determinism first — Siemens, SAP, Celonis, and the industrial buyer
Siemens Industrial Copilot generates PLC code ~60% faster, engineers still approve every line · SAP Joule reaches 34,000 customers · Celonis: $770M+ ARR, ~50–60% of the process-mining market · a chemical line cannot tolerate a hallucination the way a chat user can

Microsoft in France: Europe Still Depends on US Cloud
Gaia-X, SecNumCloud, and the gap between paper and racks
AWS + Microsoft + Google hold ~70% of Europe's cloud market; European vendors are down from ~29% (2017) to ~15% · Gaia-X, seven years in, now certifies the very hyperscalers it was built to counter · asked in the French Senate whether data could be shielded from US legal reach, a Microsoft France director answered: "No, I cannot guarantee" · CADA proposes four sovereignty assurance levels

Europe's Language Wall: A Tax and a Moat
24 official languages — a fragmentation cost for entrants, a moat for locals
~90% of Meta's Llama training corpus is English; German, French, Dutch each under 1% · DeepL won 75 of 80 blind test groups (94%) against GPT-5.2 and Google Translate · OpenEuroLLM: €37.4M, 32 languages, trained on EuroHPC · EuroLLM-22B covers 35 languages, fully open, trained on MareNostrum 5

ACCOUNT SIX · GEOPOLITICS · SEP 17, 2026
Nexperia: Europe's AI Geopolitics in One Company
A Dutch chip firm, two superpowers, and the asymmetry of chokepoints
The Netherlands froze Nexperia's global assets in Oct 2025 under a 1952 cold-war law — 70% of its output is packaged in China, and European car lines stopped within weeks · the EU imports 100% of its heavy rare earths and >90% of rare-earth processing from China · EU–China trade still reached $828.1B in 2025, up 5.4% · ~28% of European AI startups use Chinese open-weight models
America holds the chokepoint above (equipment), China the one below (materials), and Europe's industry depends on both. "De-risking" is a patchy map, not a clean front — Europe is the third party buying room between two superpowers, paying in rules and balance.

The ledger closes

One grade, six times

What holds

Strong on the soft, defensive layers

Rules, standards, certification, local context, open weights, industrial reliability. Every account lands here: AI Act compliance as a sales asset, DeepL's 94%, EuroLLM's 35 languages, deterministic copilots that run inside audited control loops. These are real moats — they keep European customers in Europe.

What doesn't

Weak on the hard, offensive layers

Compute, chips, cloud, frontier models. 20% of demand against 3–5% of supply; 70% of the cloud owned by three US companies; no indigenous training chip before 2028; no sub-7nm fab before 2030. Rules can govern how others use AI inside Europe — they cannot build a European frontier model.
